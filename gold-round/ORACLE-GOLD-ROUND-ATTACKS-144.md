# ORACLE GOLD ROUND — 144 SHARPENED THEOREM ATTACKS

## Mission

The first 200-attack arsenal opened the search surface. This round attacks the induction layer. Raw exact values remain valuable evidence, but gold requires a reusable mechanism: classification, stability, breakpoint explanation, cross-order lemma, infinite construction, all-N bound, compact proof, source-audited novelty theorem, or frozen replay victory.

Current working inputs include the verifier-backed C3 frontier with 11 at N=45,48,50 and 12 at N=52; C4 transitions through N=30; reported C5 and C6 plateaus; Sidon∩C3 exact results through N=50; C2∩C3 findings; and a large coefficient-mutation corpus. These are theorem-induction inputs, not automatic historical-priority certificates.

## Gold admission contract

- No score for finding count alone.
- A plateau must be gap-free, exact, and semantics-frozen.
- Every theorem exports its failure boundary and least counterexamples.
- External theory is untrusted until sourced, atomized, compiled, micro-tested, ablated, and independently checked.
- Replay means identical logic, sealed parameter rules, and outcome-blind second-target selection.
- Mathematical truth and historical novelty remain separate ledgers.
