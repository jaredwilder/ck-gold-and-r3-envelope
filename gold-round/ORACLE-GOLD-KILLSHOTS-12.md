# ORACLE GOLD KILLSHOTS — 12 FLAGSHIP CAMPAIGNS

A killshot is admitted only when it produces a theorem mechanism rather than another attractive table row.

## 1. OCG-133 — Gold 1 — C3 breakpoint structure

**Target:** Can Oracle explain the 11 plateau and the birth of 12?

**Execution:** Combine complete extremizer census, blocker motifs, gap/residue invariants, and proof compression; reject table-only output.

**Admission:** A multi-N structural theorem characterizing the first 12-set mechanism.

**Kill condition:** Only constants and certificates are produced.

**Operators:** all_optima, mechanism_mine, proof_compact, formalize

## 2. OCG-134 — Gold 2 — C6 plateau classification

**Target:** Can every C6 optimum on 9≤N≤15 be classified and the endpoint explained?

**Execution:** Complete all-optima enumeration, grammar induction, proof of completeness, and endpoint mechanism.

**Admission:** A classification plus plateau endpoint theorem.

**Kill condition:** Selected examples replace a census.

**Operators:** all_optima, grammar, endpoint, formalize

## 3. OCG-135 — Gold 3 — Uniform Pascal obstruction

**Target:** Can one k-parameter lemma cover C4–C6 or more?

**Execution:** Anti-unify proof motifs and solve symbolic coefficient conditions.

**Admission:** One genuine cross-order theorem.

**Kill condition:** The result is a disguised finite case list.

**Operators:** core_antiunify, symbolic_k, proof

## 4. OCG-136 — Gold 4 — Infinite Ck-free family

**Target:** Can finite evidence yield a nontrivial all-N construction?

**Execution:** Tournament every construction paradigm and formally prove the winner.

**Admission:** An explicit infinite family with a rigorous size bound.

**Kill condition:** Only finite witnesses result.

**Operators:** construction_tournament, symbolic_proof, formalize

## 5. OCG-137 — Gold 5 — Sidon–C3 mechanism

**Target:** Can non-binding be explained rather than tabulated?

**Execution:** Prove a repair map, sufficient condition, or infinite construction while separating existential and universal claims.

**Admission:** A meaningful-range or infinite interaction theorem.

**Kill condition:** Joint values through 50 are the only output.

**Operators:** all_sidon, repair, condition_proof, formalize

## 6. OCG-138 — Gold 6 — C2–C3 synergy

**Target:** Can Oracle prove a genuinely mixed theorem?

**Execution:** Find minimal mixed cores, quantify synergy, and derive an inequality or classification.

**Admission:** A theorem not implied by either marginal system.

**Kill condition:** One constraint alone explains the result.

**Operators:** mixed_core, synergy, proof

## 7. OCG-139 — Gold 7 — Coefficient universality class

**Target:** Can dozens of mutation findings collapse into one parametric theorem?

**Execution:** Build phase cells, common grammars, and proof triggers; prove one nontrivial class.

**Admission:** A theorem covering many coefficient vectors.

**Kill condition:** The class remains empirical clustering.

**Operators:** phase_arrangement, classify, uniform_proof

## 8. OCG-140 — Gold 8 — Finite-state extremal law

**Target:** Can a bounded state machine generate values, proofs, or extremizers?

**Execution:** Infer states, minimize them, prove sufficiency, and blind-predict extensions.

**Admission:** A recurrence or automaton theorem with a successful held-out prediction.

**Kill condition:** The state memorizes N.

**Operators:** state_infer, minimize, prove, blind_test

## 9. OCG-141 — Gold 9 — Finite-to-infinite upper theorem

**Target:** Can a local certified obstruction yield an all-N bound?

**Execution:** Use window averaging, container induction, or recursive certificates with explicit boundary corrections.

**Admission:** A general upper bound stronger than naive theory.

**Kill condition:** Cross-window structure is uncontrolled.

**Operators:** local_lemma, averaging, induction, formalize

## 10. OCG-142 — Gold 10 — Human-sized proof

**Target:** Can a machine optimum become a compact mathematical proof?

**Execution:** Run the proof-language tournament and require a transparent primitive ledger.

**Admission:** A proof auditable without rerunning the optimizer.

**Kill condition:** Prose hides a large case table.

**Operators:** proof_tournament, compress, audit

## 11. OCG-143 — Gold 11 — Source-audited new theorem

**Target:** Can one structural result survive a full historical-priority audit?

**Execution:** Complete primary-source and independent novelty dossiers after the theorem itself is established.

**Admission:** A defensible novelty claim with explicit coverage limits.

**Kill condition:** Priority rests on uncited model synthesis.

**Operators:** full_lit_audit, replicate, dossier

## 12. OCG-144 — Gold 12 — First attack-compounding victory

**Target:** Can one external theory seed solve two problems with zero second inference?

**Execution:** Seal seed, compile deterministic operators, prove assumptions, win target one, blind-select target two, replay identical blade, and independently certify both.

**Admission:** One immutable blade hash, two theorem receipts, and a calibrated failure boundary.

**Kill condition:** Any theory edit, manual retuning, cherry-picking, or unproved assumption occurs.

**Operators:** seed_compile, ablate, blind_select, frozen_replay, dual_certify
